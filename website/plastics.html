Types of Plastics

  • PET (Polyethylene Terephthalate): Used in water bottles, food packaging. Recyclable
  • HDPE (High-Density Polyethylene): Used in milk jugs, detergent bottles. Recyclable
  • PVC (Polyvinyl Chloride): Used in pipes, cable insulation. Limited recycling
  • LDPE (Low-Density Polyethylene): Used in plastic bags, some computer parts. Limited recycling
  • PP (Polypropylene): Used in bottle caps, straws, electronics. Recyclable
  • PS (Polystyrene): Used in foam packaging, CD cases. Not easily recyclable
  • Other (Polycarbonate, ABS, etc): Used in electronics, laptops, phones. Check locally
