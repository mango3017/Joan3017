What is E-Plastic?

E-plastic, or electronic plastic, refers to the plastic components found in discarded electronic devices such as computers, mobile phones, televisions, printers, and other gadgets. These plastics are often mixed with metals, glass, and hazardous substances, making their recycling and disposal a significant environmental challenge. E-plastics can contain a variety of polymers, including ABS, polycarbonate, and PVC, and may also be treated with flame retardants and other additives. Proper management of e-plastic waste is crucial to prevent environmental pollution, conserve resources, and reduce the health risks associated with improper disposal.

What is Data Mining?

Data mining is the process of discovering patterns, trends, and useful information from large sets of data using statistical, mathematical, and computational techniques. In the context of e-plastic management, data mining helps organizations and policymakers analyze waste generation, recycling rates, and material flows. By extracting actionable insights from complex datasets, data mining supports better decision-making, optimizes recycling processes, and enables the development of effective strategies for sustainable e-waste management. Ultimately, data mining transforms raw data into valuable knowledge that drives innovation and environmental stewardship.
